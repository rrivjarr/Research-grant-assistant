ID: SERB-CRG
Title: SERB Core Research Grant (CRG)
Agency: SERB (DST)
Source: https://prism.serbonline.in/RS-CRG
Retrieved: 2026-11-25
Note: Paraphrased from the SERB scheme page and the DST schemes page (indiascienceandtechnology.gov.in). Verify against the original.
Deadline: not stated (check the current call notice)
Budget: no fixed upper or lower limit; the budget should match the work proposed
Duration: 3 years
Themes: core research support for active researchers in frontier areas of science and engineering; all science and engineering disciplines; judged on novelty and the investigator's competence
Eligibility:
- Applicant must hold a regular academic or research position at a recognised academic institution, national laboratory or R&D institution in India
- An investigator can hold only one such project at a time
Proposal format:
- Research proposal
- Plagiarism undertaking
Attachments:
- Plagiarism undertaking
