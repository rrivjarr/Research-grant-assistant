Agency: DBT

Retrieved: 2026-11-25
Note: Paraphrased summary of the announcement page. Verify against the original before relying on it.
Deadline: 2026-09-23
Budget: not stated
Duration: up to 3 years
Themes: increase participation of women researchers and support their career development in biotechnology
Eligibility:
- Applicant must be a woman
- Applicant must be an Indian national residing in India
- Applicant age must be up to 55 years
- Applicant must be unemployed on the closing date
- Applicant must hold a PhD in biotechnology, life sciences or an allied field, or an MD, MDS or MVSc degree, or an MTech in biotechnology or an allied field or an MPharm degree
- Candidates currently enrolled in higher studies or receiving any fellowship are not eligible

- Research proposal
