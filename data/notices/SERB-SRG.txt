ID: SERB-SRG
Title: SERB Start-up Research Grant (SRG)
Agency: SERB (DST)
Source: https://prism.serbonline.in/RS-NSRG
Retrieved: 2026-11-25
Note: Paraphrased summary of the official scheme page. Verify against the original before relying on it.
Deadline: not stated (announced per call on serbonline.in)
Budget: Rs 30 lakh plus overheads
Duration: 2 years
Themes: frontier areas of science and engineering; helps researchers start an independent research career at a new institution; stepping stone to the Core Research Grant
Eligibility:
- Applicant must be an Indian citizen
- Applicant must hold a PhD in Science or Engineering, or an MD, MS, MDS or MVSc degree
- Applicant must hold a regular academic or research position at a recognised institution; contractual or temporary roles do not qualify
- Applicant must apply within two years of joining the institution
- Upper age limit is 42 years, with a 3 year relaxation for SC, ST, OBC, persons with disabilities and women
- Applicants who already received a SERB grant such as YSS, ECR or CRG are not eligible
- Co-PIs are not permitted
Proposal format:
- Project title [max 500 chars]
- Project summary [max 3000 chars]
- Keywords [max 6 items]
- Objectives [max 1500 chars]
- Expected outputs and outcomes [max 1500 chars]
- Budget by head (manpower, consumables, travel, equipment, contingency, overheads)
- Technical details PDF
Attachments:
- Biodata
- PI certificate
- Endorsement letter from head of institution
- Age proof
