ID: ICMR-NMC-MEDEDU
Title: ICMR and NMC Joint Call: Research for Strengthening and Enhancing Quality of Medical Education (2026)
Agency: ICMR with NMC
Source: https://www.icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/Call/1781779099_jointcallicmrnmc-strentheningmedicaleducation-e-office18062026.pdf
Retrieved: 2026-11-25
Note: Paraphrased summary of the call document. Verify against the original before relying on it.
Deadline: 2026-08-21
Budget: no per-project ceiling; budget must follow ICMR guidelines with justification for each item
Duration: up to 3 years (small grant) or up to 4 years (intermediate grant); optional 6 month preparatory phase
Themes: medical education; competency based medical education; digital and technology enabled teaching of scientific knowledge; building research skills and competencies of undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral students
Eligibility:
- Applicant institution must be a medical college or medical institution, with the head of the institution endorsing the project
- Principal investigator must be regular faculty holding an MBBS, MD, MS or DM degree with some prior research effort in medical education
- At least one Co-PI must be from the PI's institution; up to three Co-PIs are allowed
- ICMR scientists cannot apply as PI
Proposal format:
- Title
- Structured summary [max 500 words]
- Keywords
- Background and rationale [max 1000 words]
- Research questions
- Objectives [max 4 items]
- Methodology
- Implementation plan with milestone chart
- Risks and mitigation
- Expected outcomes [max 500 words]
- Next steps after the project
- AI-use disclosure
- References
- Preliminary work [max 1000 words]
- Team expertise
- Institutional support
- Detailed budget
Attachments:
- Proposal
- CVs of PI and Co-PIs
- Endorsement letter from head of institution
- Ethics clearance or proof of submission
- Budget breakdown
