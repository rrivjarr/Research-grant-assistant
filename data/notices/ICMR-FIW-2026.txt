ID: ICMR-FIW-2026
Title: ICMR First in the World Challenge Grants (2026)
Agency: ICMR
Source: https://www.icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/Call/1790846907_call_fiw_2026.pdf
Retrieved: 2026-11-25
Note: Paraphrased summary of the call document. Verify against the original before relying on it.
Deadline: 2026-11-30
Budget: Type A proof of concept up to Rs 1 crore; Type B prototype up to Rs 4 crore; Type C final product up to Rs 8 crore
Duration: up to 4 years (Type A 2 years, Type B 3 years, Type C 4 years)
Themes: high risk high reward first of its kind biomedical ideas such as new vaccines, drugs, diagnostics, devices and interventions; health technologies; incremental knowledge and process only innovations are excluded
Eligibility:
- Applicants may be individuals or teams; a team must name one PI
- Applicant institution must be a government medical college, university, recognised R&D lab, NGO, startup, MSME or industry
- At least one Co-PI must be from the PI's institute
Proposal format:
- Title [max 25 words]
- Structured summary [max 350 words]
- Keywords [max 6 items]
- Problem statement [max 500 words]
- Rationale [max 250 words]
- Objectives [max 4 items]
- Significance [max 750 words]
- Methodology [max 1000 words]
- Work plan with GANTT or PERT chart
- Expected outcomes [max 200 words]
- Future plans [max 100 words]
- Conflict of interest [max 30 words]
- IP status [max 30 words]
- Institutional support facilities and manpower
- Preliminary work [max 250 words]
- Team expertise
- Laboratory facilities
- Detailed budget with justification
Attachments:
- Short CVs of PI and Co-PIs
- Supplementary PDF
- Supporting cost documents for equipment
