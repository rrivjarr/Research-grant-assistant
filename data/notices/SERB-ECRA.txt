ID: SERB-ECRA
Title: SERB Early Career Research Award (ECRA)
Agency: SERB (DST)
Source: https://prism.serbonline.in/RS-ECRA
Retrieved: 2026-11-25
Note: Paraphrased summary of the official scheme page. Verify against the original before relying on it.
Deadline: not stated (check the current call notice)
Budget: up to Rs 50 lakh excluding overheads
Duration: 3 years
Themes: quick research support for early career researchers pursuing innovative work in frontier areas of science and engineering
Eligibility:
- Applicant must hold a PhD in Science or Engineering, or an MD or MS in a medical field
- Applicant must hold a regular academic or research position at a recognised institution; applicants without a finalised post are not eligible
- Upper age limit is 37 years, with a 3 year relaxation for SC, ST, OBC, persons with disabilities and women
- Co-PIs and mentors are not allowed
Proposal format:
- Research proposal
Attachments:
- Mandatory documents listed on the portal (not captured here)
